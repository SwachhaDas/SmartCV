How to Use SmartCV

Follow these simple steps to build your professional portfolio and CV.

Create an Account

Register an account to get started. Once logged in, you will have access to your personal Dashboard.

Add Your Information

Go to the Dashboard and add your Education, Experience, Skills, Projects, and Social Links. Make sure to fill them out completely.

Choose a Template

Go to your Profile Settings. You can choose different designs for your public portfolio, and even turn it into a full website!

Generate & Download CV

Click "Generate CV" on your dashboard. Select whether you are applying for an Academic, Job, or Faculty position, and we will generate an ATS-friendly PDF for you.
